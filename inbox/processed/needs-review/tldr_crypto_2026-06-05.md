# DOJ Anti-Scam Operation 👮, SpaceX Pre-IPO Perpetual Futures 📈, Spacecoin DePIN Satellites 🛰️

## Summary
This TLDR Crypto newsletter from June 5, 2026 captures a pivotal convergence of law enforcement, institutional finance, and decentralized infrastructure in the crypto ecosystem. Three major themes dominate: (1) A coordinated DOJ-led anti-scam operation that mobilized major tech and crypto companies—Coinbase, SpaceX, Meta, Apple, Google, Microsoft, TRM Labs—alongside international law enforcement to disrupt Southeast Asia-based scam networks, resulting in $3.8M in frozen crypto, 63 arrests, termination of Starlink kits used by scammers, and disruption of 1.4M+ accounts. This signals a new era of public-private collaboration in crypto enforcement. (2) Coinbase launched SPCX-PERP, a perpetual futures contract tracking SpaceX's pre-IPO valuation (~$75B target at $135/share), offering up to 5x leverage, USDC settlement, and 24/7 trading—with automatic conversion to standard perps if SpaceX goes public. This represents a significant expansion of crypto derivatives into pre-IPO equity markets, with more contracts across tech/AI/energy/space planned. (3) Spacecoin signed a $100M MoU with Vietnam's DETI Technology to deploy decentralized satellite communications, sovereign routing, blockchain, and edge AI infrastructure—a major DePIN (Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Network) milestone. Additional notable developments include Mastercard expanding onchain settlement to 6 stablecoins across 8 blockchains, Ethereum's LeanSig initiative exploring post-quantum signatures, Binance's tokenized equities (bStocks) raising ownership rights concerns, Arthur Hayes liquidating $18M+ in HYPE and NEAR holdings days after public bullish bets, BTC dropping below $70K amid $714M+ liquidations, and Deel launching a stablecoin wallet for global contractors.

- Public-private crypto enforcement coalitions are scaling: The DOJ operation shows that crypto compliance is no longer siloed—exchanges (Coinbase), satellite providers (Starlink/SpaceX), tech platforms (Meta, Apple, Google), and analytics firms (TRM Labs) are now integrated into cross-border law enforcement workflows. For anyone building in crypto, compliance-by-design and proactive law enforcement collaboration is becoming table stakes, not optional.
- Pre-IPO perpetual futures are a new asset class: Coinbase's SPCX-PERP lets traders gain leveraged exposure to private company valuations (SpaceX at ~$75B) with USDC settlement, 24/7 trading, and auto-conversion upon IPO. This blurs the line between crypto derivatives and traditional equity markets. The planned expansion to tech/AI/energy/space pre-IPO contracts suggests a broader trend of tokenizing private market access.
- DePIN is moving from theory to sovereign infrastructure deals: Spacecoin's $100M MoU with Vietnam's DETI for decentralized satellite internet, sovereign routing, and edge AI is one of the largest DePIN-government partnerships to date. This validates the DePIN thesis at national-infrastructure scale and signals that governments are open to decentralized alternatives for communications and data sovereignty.
- Stablecoins are becoming institutional settlement rails: Mastercard supporting 6 stablecoins across 8 blockchains for 24/7 settlement, plus banks being urged to treat stablecoins as revenue opportunities (on/off-ramps) rather than just deposit threats, shows stablecoins are being absorbed into traditional finance infrastructure. Tokenized deposits and stablecoins are expected to coexist.

This is a cautionary signal for anyone building tokenized asset platforms—regulatory and ownership structure clarity is critical.

### How Ethereum Plans to Replace BLS with Post-Quantum Signatures
- **URL:** https://hashcloak.com/blog/how-ethereum-plans-to-replace-bls-with-post-quantum-signatures?utm_source=tldrcrypto
- **Via:** TLDR Crypto, 2026-06-05
- **Read time:** 5 minute read
- **TLDR Summary:** The Lean Ethereum team is working on replacing BLS signatures, which underpin consensus across Ethereum's ~1 million validators but are vulnerable to Shor's algorithm on quantum computers, with a hash-based scheme called LeanSig. LeanSig builds on XMSS and Merkle trees to give validators a stateful many-time signing structure that uses only hash functions rather than elliptic curve pairings, making it quantum-resistant by design. Aggregation of the resulting signatures (which can't be combined algebraically the way BLS can) is handled by a custom hash-based zkVM called leanVM, inspired by Cairo and formally verified using the Lean proof assistant.

[How Ethereum Plans to Replace BLS with Post-Quantum Signatures (5 minute read)](https://hashcloak.com/blog/how-ethereum-plans-to-replace-bls-with-post-quantum-signatures?utm_source=tldrcrypto) The Lean Ethereum team is working on replacing BLS signatures, which underpin consensus across Ethereum's ~1 million validators but are vulnerable to Shor's algorithm on quantum computers, with a hash-based scheme called LeanSig. LeanSig builds on XMSS and Merkle trees to give validators a stateful many-time signing structure that uses only hash functions rather than elliptic curve pairings, making it quantum-resistant by design. Aggregation of the resulting signatures (which can't be combined algebraically the way BLS can) is handled by a custom hash-based zkVM called leanVM, inspired by Cairo and formally verified using the Lean proof assistant.
