The full rollout is expected later this year, with testing currently available in Firefox Nightly builds.

iOS 27 could make it far easier to manage your AirPods (1 minute read) Apple is reportedly planning a major redesign of the AirPods settings experience in iOS 27, iPadOS 27, and macOS 27, making advanced features like adaptive audio, gesture controls, and hearing tools easier to find and manage as AirPods evolve into more sophisticated wearable devices — though a standalone AirPods app still may not be coming.

Apple Intelligence image models to boast ‘major' visual upgrades in iOS 27 (1 minute read) Apple is reportedly giving its Genmoji and Image Playground AI image generation models a major quality upgrade in iOS 27 after widespread criticism of the low-quality results introduced in iOS 18.2, especially compared to competing AI tools. The update may also expand Image Playground beyond Apple's own on-device models and ChatGPT integration to support additional third-party image generators, potentially including Google's Nano Banana models, while new features like photo library–based Genmoji suggestions are also expected.

The Design System Advantage Is Memory (12 minute read) The real advantage in AI-powered design systems is not the number of tools or the sophistication of the model, but whether agents can access and reason over a company's accumulated decision-making memory — including design critiques, Slack discussions, ADRs, deprecated patterns, and research history. As AI agents take on longer and more complex tasks, poor context retrieval becomes increasingly expensive in both compute costs and human oversight, leading to repeated corrections and lost trust. The proposed solution is to structure organizational knowledge into searchable indexes or graph-based systems that connect components, decisions, owners, and outcomes, allowing agents to retrieve the right context before acting instead of repeatedly rediscovering it.

When Designers Start Building (4 minute read) Automattic ran a workshop where twenty designers learned to work directly in production codebases instead of just handing off designs to engineering teams. The goal was to close the gap between design and final product by having designers build with real components and APIs rather than just creating mockups in Figma. By the end, several designers had opened their first pull requests and were making design decisions directly in the codebase, representing a shift toward designers working closer to the actual product.

How Designers Can Handle Finance Stuff Without Losing Creative Flow (3 minute read) Designers often struggle with finance tasks because they require a completely different mindset than creative work, disrupting their flow. The solution is to batch administrative tasks into dedicated "office hours" and automate repetitive processes like payment tracking and invoicing. As design businesses grow and add team members, having professional financial systems becomes crucial for maintaining trust and allowing everyone to focus on creative work.

Fraude Design (Website) Fraude Design is a satirical AI-free website builder that parodies poor design decisions and technical debt creation. It mocks founders who make bad design choices while convincing themselves their work is good, targeting CEOs who want to create subpar websites without effort.

MagicPath (Website) MagicPath turns design into a multiplayer AI workspace where teams build and iterate on live, interactive browser-based interfaces together with no design-to-engineering handoff.

Turn Your Code Into Stunning Videos (Website) Repoclip is an AI-powered tool that automatically converts GitHub repositories into professional demo videos within 60 seconds by analyzing code structure and generating scripts, visuals, and narration.

Seven Tips for Using Figma Make Credits More Efficiently (11 minute read) Figma Make now offers Make kits and Make attachments that provide real components, data, and constraints for more contextual prototyping. These new features give users greater control and context when building prototypes within Figma Make. The updates aim to make the prototyping process more efficient by starting with actual design elements rather than generic placeholders.

Bold, optimistic, empathetic: How a retirement company perfected its look for Gen Z (3 minute read) Standard Life worked with Conran Design Group to modernize its 200-year-old brand and make retirement planning feel more relevant and approachable to younger audiences by introducing a bolder, more optimistic identity, updated digital-first visuals, conversational messaging, and a new “Journey Line” brand asset symbolizing the ups and downs of retirement. The rebrand aimed to shift perceptions of pensions from intimidating and passive to empowering and human, while preserving the trust and heritage expected from a long-established financial institution.

AI UX Design: Strategic Blueprint for the AI-augmented Designer (16 minute read) AI has transformed UX design from manual execution to strategic curation, where designers now direct AI tools rather than create everything manually. The shift moves designers from "pixel-pushing" to problem-solving, focusing on defining the "why" while AI handles the "how" through accelerated workflows. AI-augmented designers use a three-phase framework: setting human context, AI-driven exploration, and human validation to maintain quality control.

In the Age of AI, Design Instinct and Experience Matter More than Ever (5 minute read) Generative AI is lowering the barrier to entry in design, but rather than replacing human creatives, it is exposing how much the craft depends on instinct and taste built over years of experience.
