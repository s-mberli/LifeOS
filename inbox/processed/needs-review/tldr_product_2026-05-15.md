Product-market fit is a trap (5 minute read) In AI, product-market fit can become a trap when companies over-optimize around what worked last quarter while customer expectations keep changing. Winning teams will stay lean, close to customers, and willing to replace their own product before the market moves past them.

Why AI Initiatives Break Normal Product Manager Instincts (6 minute read) AI initiatives quickly turn normal product work into transformation work by exposing messy workflows, unclear ownership, and weak governance. The best PMs keep momentum by starting with narrow, low-risk pilots, learning quickly, and adding structure only once the workflow proves repeatable.

Beyond the Coding Assistant: A Series on AI-Assisted Software Engineering (3 minute read) AI coding assistants improve individual developer speed, but they do not automatically improve team delivery. The next frontier is lifecycle orchestration across the full software development process, helping the entire team work faster, cheaper, and more effectively.

PM's Guide to Claude - When to use Chat vs Cowork vs Code (93 minute video) PM work is shifting from one-off AI chats to persistent agent systems that can access files, use tools, learn from feedback, and run across devices. The best PMs will build self-improving workflows in Claude Cowork, Claude Code, and Dispatch, using AI to orchestrate work instead of repeatedly prompting from scratch.

SEO is a product, AEO is brand (5 minute read) SEO should operate like a product function, with the authority to coordinate engineering, content, and long-term roadmap work. AEO belongs with the brand because AI visibility depends less on technical optimization and more on reputation, authority, narrative, and what the broader internet says about the company.

How to Calculate the Inference Efficiency Ratio (7 minute read) Inference costs are becoming a major threat to AI product margins, and traditional SaaS metrics do not isolate the problem quickly enough. The Inference Efficiency Ratio helps teams measure AI revenue against token spend, spot inefficient usage, and protect margins through better pricing, routing, caching, and launch gates.

OpenAI Symphony - From Prompts to Plays (2 minute read) OpenAI's Symphony points to a future where product teams manage agents through task queues instead of manually prompting models.

Omaha as judgment day for AGI (3 minute read) The real test for foundation labs is whether they can become durable, cash-generating businesses rather than capital-hungry revolutionary bets.
