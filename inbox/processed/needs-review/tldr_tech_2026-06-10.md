Apple's open-source container tool for Mac, built on the Containerization Swift package with OCI compatibility, lowers the barrier for developers to run Linux containers natively on macOS. Combined with Apple's broader AI strategy of embedding intelligence into iPhone/Siri rather than competing on infrastructure capex, this positions Apple to win consumer AI by default through ecosystem integration.

GM's sodium-ion battery development targets AI data centers and grid storage, promising simpler, cooler operation without active cooling — a direct response to the energy crisis AI is creating. WorkOS's auth.md protocol enables AI agents to programmatically register and authenticate with services, addressing a critical gap in the agent ecosystem.

Both address the same problem: AI's energy demands are outpacing traditional infrastructure.

This solves a real gap in the agent ecosystem — how agents discover and authenticate with third-party services without human intervention.
