This strategy positions Block to own the “agent layer” of financial services, where control over data and workflows compounds into a durable advantage.

By shifting from record-keeping to action-oriented automation, the company is positioning itself to redefine how financial operations and advisory services are delivered at scale.

Coastal Bank teams with Tempo to transform cross-border money movement (5 minute read) Coastal Bank in the US is building stablecoin-settled payment infrastructure on Tempo for its fintech clients, pairing existing institutional messaging with onchain settlement to move money faster with fewer intermediaries.

Kraken parent Payward to acquire Bitnomial for up to $550m (2 minute read) Kraken's parent company is moving deeper into regulated crypto markets with a planned acquisition of derivatives platform Bitnomial in a deal worth up to $550 million. The move brings valuable CFTC-licensed infrastructure in-house, giving Payward a faster path to offering compliant US-based derivatives products like perpetuals and options. It also positions the company to expand its B2B offering, enabling fintechs and financial institutions to plug into regulated crypto trading through a single integration.

AmEx to buy Altman-backed Hyper in push for AI-powered expense tools (2 minute read) American Express is acquiring AI startup Hyper to deepen its push into automating corporate spending workflows. Hyper, backed by OpenAI CEO Sam Altman, focuses on streamlining expense management tasks like approvals, compliance checks, and reporting through AI. The move highlights how incumbents are embedding AI directly into financial operations to reduce manual work and improve efficiency at scale.

Visa and Mastercard fee settlement faces pushback from merchants (3 minute read) A proposed settlement to reduce interchange fees from Visa and Mastercard faces strong opposition from major retailers like Walmart, who argue it fails to introduce real competition or structural reform. While the deal includes modest fee reductions and new merchant flexibility, critics say it preserves the core dynamics that keep fees high and limit negotiation power. The outcome could shape both regulatory pressure and future legislation around payment network competition.

LendingClub to rebrand as Happen Bank (3 minute read) Reflecting its evolution from a peer-to-peer lending platform into a full-service digital bank.

Middle East leapfrogs to open finance to drive financial inclusion (5 minute read) Unlike Western markets, the Middle East is aligning banks and fintechs around inclusion-focused ecosystems.
