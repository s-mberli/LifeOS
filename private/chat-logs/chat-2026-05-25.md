The poster stumbled upon NASA's "Power of Ten" paper by Gerard Holzmann (2006), designed for safety-critical C code, and found its principles highly relevant to AI-generated code. The core philosophy is that **code should be mechanically verifiable, not just functional.**

**Core Principle: Mechanical Verifiability**
The fundamental idea is that code should be structured and written in such a way that its correctness and safety can be proven, either by automated tools or a human, even under pressure. This goes beyond mere functionality to ensure robustness and predictability.
