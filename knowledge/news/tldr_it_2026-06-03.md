Salesforce Signs Definitive Agreement to Acquire Contentful (2 minute read) Salesforce is acquiring Contentful to add a native, enterprise-grade content layer to its AI CRM stack, strengthening its ability to deliver personalized, AI-generated experiences across channels. The deal plugs Contentful's headless CMS and APIs into Customer 360 and Agentforce, letting enterprises dynamically assemble content using customer data, context, and business rules at scale.

Apple has released macOS Tahoe 26.5.1 (2 minute read) Apple has released a patch update for macOS Tahoe, bringing it to version 26.5.1. The update addresses an enterprise issue where Mac computers with an M5 chip could unexpectedly shut down when using certain content-filtering network extensions, and Apple reports no published CVE entries. The Apple silicon download is about 2.14 GB, and the article suggests most users may not need to install unless they're on an M5 model in an enterprise setting.

ERP vendors have lost control of the narrative. What's the story? (4 minute read) ERP vendors have lost the plot. Instead of selling measurable business value, they're flooding the market with AI buzzwords like agents, copilots, and platforms. Companies still buy ERP based on ROI, risk, and operational impact, but AI costs, governance, staffing, and pricing are still too unclear for vendors to prove real value at scale.

At Sapphire, SAP makes the case that enterprise AI is a context problem (8 minute read) At SAP Sapphire 2026, SAP said enterprise AI is not about better chatbots or models, but about giving AI agents the right business context, data, and governance to actually work inside real enterprise systems. To support this, SAP rolled out its Business AI Platform, combining its ERP foundation, data tools, and new agent management capabilities into one stack aimed at controlling and coordinating AI across the business.

Enterprise Software Leaders Build AI Agents With NVIDIA (7 minute read) NVIDIA announced the NVIDIA Agent Toolkit for building secure, long-running enterprise AI agents, including NVIDIA NemoClaw blueprints, Nemotron open models, the OpenShell secure runtime, and CUDA-X libraries exposed as agent “skills.” Early adopters such as Cadence, Dassault Systèmes, Siemens, Synopsys, CrowdStrike, and Palantir are using these components to automate engineering workflows and cybersecurity/operations tasks, while partners (Microsoft, Canonical, Red Hat, SAP, ServiceNow) integrate OpenShell for policy- and privacy-controlled deployments across Windows, servers, and enterprise platforms.

Cisco Launches Refreshed Hardware Procurement Platform (3 minute read) Cisco introduced Shop Cisco Refresh, a US e-commerce platform built with TD SYNNEX to help partners source Cisco-certified remanufactured equipment with real-time inventory and pricing. Useful for lean IT teams looking for warrantied Cisco gear at lower cost, though the announcement is more procurement/channel focused than broad IT news.

Netskope Launches AI Command Center to Track Enterprise AI Sprawl (4 minute read) Netskope introduced AI Command Center, a new capability in its Netskope One SASE platform to help companies discover, assess, govern, and secure AI apps, models, and agents across the enterprise. The platform is designed to show both approved and unsanctioned AI usage, connect activity back to users, apps, data, and policies, and automate remediation when risky behavior is detected.

What is modern EDR? The upgrade to enterprise-grade endpoint protection (3 minute read) Modern EDR (endpoint detection and response) upgrades traditional antivirus by adding continuous monitoring, AI-driven behavioral detection, and automated response to catch and contain modern attacks like ransomware and fileless malware. It focuses on turning noisy alerts into clear, contextual incidents so even SMEs and MSPs can get enterprise-grade protection without needing large security teams.
