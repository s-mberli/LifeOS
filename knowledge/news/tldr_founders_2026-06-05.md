### How to Build an Outcome-Based Pricing Plan

### How to influence complex buying groups
- **URL:** https://gtmsos.substack.com/p/b2b-buying-group-messaging-strategy?utm_source=tldrfounders
- **Via:** TLDR Founders, 2026-06-05
- **Read time:** 5 minute read
- **TLDR Summary:** One excited champion isn't enough to close a deal anymore. Even a mid-sized sale now has to get past product, finance, security, legal, and the execs, and each of them cares about something different. Product wants clarity, finance wants the numbers to work, security wants to know it's safe, and your champion wants to not look bad for picking you. The trick is to tell one clear story and adjust it for each person, instead of using seven different pitches. Instead of asking buyers which message they like, look at how your existing customers actually decided to buy.

### Google Search adding profile pages for websites and creators
- **URL:** https://9to5google.com/2026/06/04/google-search-profiles/?utm_source=tldrfounders
- **Via:** TLDR Founders, 2026-06-05
- **Read time:** 2 minute read
- **TLDR Summary:** Google's new 'Search profiles' allow website owners and creators to shape their own presence on Search. Search profiles can be accessed via a creator or publisher's Knowledge Panel, Discover feed, or through a direct URL. They can contain page and social media links, videos, and posts. More capabilities will be introduced in the coming months. The feature is coming first to the US before further expanding to more publishers and creators worldwide.

### Asana Launches Agentic Work Management Platform, Pitching Itself as the ‘Easy Button' for AI-Driven Enterprises
- **URL:** https://thelettertwo.com/2026/06/04/asana-agentic-work-management-platform/?utm_source=tldrfounders
- **Via:** TLDR Founders, 2026-06-05
- **Read time:** 9 minute read
- **TLDR Summary:** Agents fail in the enterprise due to a lack of organizational memory and understanding of roles and responsibilities. Asana has been building the infrastructure that fixes these issues even before agents existed to use it. Its Agentic Work Management Platform is a system designed to align humans and agents around the same plan, context, and governance. It is composed of a chief-of-staff agent called Dash, a new generation of AI Teammates, and three vertical applications that target IT, software development, and professional services.
