### Amazon S3 annotations: attach rich, queryable context directly to your objects
- **URL:** https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws/amazon-s3-annotations-attach-rich-queryable-context-directly-to-your-objects/?utm_source=tldrit
- **Via:** TLDR IT, 2026-06-18
- **Read time:** 6 minute read
- **TLDR Summary:** S3 annotations is a new Amazon S3 metadata capability that lets customers attach up to 1,000 named annotations per object (up to 1 MB each) with rich formats like JSON, XML, YAML, or plain text. Annotations are mutable (can be updated or deleted without rewriting the object) and can be surfaced at scale through S3 Metadata annotation tables queryable with Amazon Athena and Iceberg engines.
