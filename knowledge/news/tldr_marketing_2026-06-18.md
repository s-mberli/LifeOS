### ChatGPT's Product Recommendations Change 80.2% When Search is Enabled vs Disabled (Study of 20,000 Responses)
- **URL:** https://visibilitylabs.com/blog/chatgpt-search-vs-no-search-product-recommendations/?utm_source=tldrmarketing
- **Via:** TLDR Marketing, 2026-06-18
- **Read time:** 3 minute read
- **TLDR Summary:** ChatGPT's product recommendations depend heavily on whether search is enabled, which means brands should focus less on influencing AI training data and more on influencing the sources AI cites. In a study of 20,000 responses, 80.2% of recommended products changed when search was enabled, and only 15.8% of products that appeared in 100% of non-search responses also appeared when search was enabled. The findings suggest that cited web content plays a major role in recommendation outcomes. Brands should focus on earning mentions in the articles, reviews, and comparison pages that ChatGPT already cites for high-value product queries.

### How Paid Media Accelerates AI Visibility
- **URL:** https://foundationinc.co/lab/paid-media-ai-visibility/?utm_source=tldrmarketing
- **Via:** TLDR Marketing, 2026-06-18
- **Read time:** 5 minute read
- **TLDR Summary:** Paid media now plays a critical role in AI visibility because it helps you identify winning messages and audience signals faster than any other channel. ### The 95/5 Playbook for LinkedIn Growth
- **URL:** https://pierreherubel.substack.com/p/the-955-playbook-for-linkedin-growth?utm_source=tldrmarketing
- **Via:** TLDR Marketing, 2026-06-18
- **Read time:** 4 minute read
- **TLDR Summary:** Most B2B LinkedIn content fails at one of two extremes: too dense for a social feed or too shallow to earn professional credibility. ### People prefer products with fewer ingredients
- **URL:** https://app.sciencesays.com/p/people-prefer-products-with-fewer-ingredients?utm_source=tldrmarketing
- **Via:** TLDR Marketing, 2026-06-18
- **Read time:** 3 minute read
- **TLDR Summary:** Consumers associate fewer ingredients with products that feel more natural, healthier, and less processed, making ingredient simplicity a strong messaging lever for food and beverage brands. Across 19 experiments, framing a product as having "few ingredients" increased choice rates by 21.6% for an identical granola bar and generated up to 44.2% higher click-through rates in Meta ads. Brands should prominently highlight low ingredient counts on packaging, ads, and product pages, especially for health-focused products where differentiation is difficult. The tactic is less effective for products bought for indulgence, flavor variety, or nutritional complexity, so brands should match the message to the purchase motivation.

### Hinge's chief marketer on why community is more than a marketing channel
- **URL:** https://www.marketingdive.com/news/hinges-chief-marketer-on-why-community-is-more-than-a-marketing-channel/823037/?utm_source=tldrmarketing
- **Via:** TLDR Marketing, 2026-06-18
- **Read time:** 3 minute read
- **TLDR Summary:** Hinge is building brand credibility with Gen Z by funding the social infrastructure its users depend on rather than treating community as a marketing channel. The ‘One More Hour' initiative has distributed more than $2M in grants to grassroots social groups across New York, Los Angeles, Atlanta, and London, with $1.5M more planned globally this year, requiring no promotion or app downloads from recipients. The strategy is a deliberate contrast to brands pulling back from purpose-driven marketing, grounded in a real behavioral gap where Gen Z spends 1,000 fewer hours of in-person connection per year than peers did 20 years ago.

### The AI notetaker winning by not joining your meeting
- **URL:** https://strategybreakdowns.com/p/granola-notepad?utm_source=tldrmarketing
- **Via:** TLDR Marketing, 2026-06-18
- **Read time:** 3 minute read
- **TLDR Summary:** Granola grew by removing friction instead of adding features. It avoided meeting bots and recorded audio at the operating system level, which let it work across Zoom, Meet, Teams, phone calls, and other audio sources without integrations. Its note taking workflow increased trust by letting users create lightweight notes that AI expands into useful summaries, which made the output feel personal and worth revisiting. Granola also built habit loops through simple product design such as one click meeting notifications that launch the call, open the app, and start transcription. After earning trust as a notetaker, it turned meeting data into a searchable knowledge base through chat, shared team spaces, and reusable prompts, which expanded its value beyond note taking and helped drive word of mouth growth, 70%+ weekly retention, and a $1.5B valuation.
