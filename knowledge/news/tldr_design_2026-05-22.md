# TLDR Design — 2026-05-22

Google Pomelli Can Now Build Your Entire Brand from Scratch (2 minute read) Google Pomelli, an AI marketing tool from Google Labs built with DeepMind, now lets small businesses build a complete brand identity from scratch through a new agentic feature. Beyond its existing social media and ad creation capabilities, the update introduces brand books and a one-click website builder. Together, these tools allow businesses with no online presence to establish their brand, run social campaigns, and launch a professional site without expensive outside help.

Canva and Adobe are Coming to Gemini, and They Want to Make Everything Chatty (2 minute read) Both Adobe and Canva are integrating with Google Gemini, allowing creative work to begin via chat prompts before users open any design app. Canva's Connected App is already rolling out in select English-language markets, enabling users to generate, edit, and layer designs directly from Gemini, while Adobe's connector arrives in the coming weeks with a broader focus on image, design, and video production through Firefly and Creative Cloud. The deeper question is whether Google will emerge as a gatekeeper for creative decisions, gaining influence over the first choice while both companies compete to stay relevant once the work starts outside their own platforms.

The Figma Design Agent is Here (8 minute read) Figma launched a native design agent that lives directly on the canvas, giving teams deep access to their components, tokens, and design system without switching tools. It supports parallel prompting, bulk edits, and feedback processing — handling repetitive tasks while keeping designers in control of the craft. Currently rolling out in beta at no credit cost, it will be available to Full seat users on Professional, Organization, and Enterprise plans upon general availability.

The waiting problem in AI products (8 minute read) Modern AI products and agents have largely ignored decades-old usability principles showing that users lose focus when systems respond too slowly, relying instead on vague spinners and minimal feedback that force people to invent coping behaviors like repeatedly checking tabs, refreshing pages, or monitoring long-running tasks themselves. AI tools should adopt the same progress indicators, ETAs, notifications, and detailed logs that operating systems and professional software have used for years to make long waits feel manageable and transparent.

Most AI tools make users faster. The best AI tools make users better (11 minute read) AI tools are increasingly taking over complex tasks, but the real challenge isn't making AI flawless — it's designing systems that keep users engaged, capable, and able to catch mistakes when the AI fails. Drawing on decades of research in automation and cognitive ergonomics, good human–AI collaboration requires balancing automation with human control, carefully calibrating user trust, and designing experiences that strengthen rather than erode people's long-term skills and judgment. Instead of training users to blindly accept outputs or repeatedly “pull the slot machine lever” with prompts like “fix this,” the best AI systems should help users stay thoughtful, informed, and actively involved in decision-making.

Small By Design: The Strength of Lean Design-system Teams (8 minute read) Small design-system teams of 2-5 people are the norm across all company sizes and can operate more effectively than larger teams when this is a strategic choice. These lean teams move faster because they have shared context, fewer bottlenecks, and can make decisions quickly without formal handoffs or extensive coordination. The small size enables better collaboration through blurred roles, forces strategic prioritization, and allows teams to scale their impact through contribution models rather than headcount.

Website Builder for WordPress (Website) Kirki is a freeform visual website builder for WordPress that allows users to place content anywhere on an infinite canvas without traditional layout restrictions.

Agentic UI Rendering Platform (Website) Montage is an agentic UI rendering platform designed to create user interfaces through autonomous agents. The platform enables intelligent automation in interface development by using AI-driven agents to handle rendering processes.

How Kit Studio drew on St. John's walls for an anti-bland rebrand (10 minute read) Modern branding has become increasingly bland and simplified due to digital optimization, often stripping historic identities of texture and personality. In rebranding St. John's College, Kit Studio pushed back against that trend by drawing from the college's real architecture, archives, colors, and craftsmanship, creating detailed hand-drawn designs that preserve warmth and heritage while still working across modern digital platforms.

Design Engineering Then vs. Now: How AI is Reshaping the Designer-developer Divide (9 minute read) AI is reshaping design engineering by introducing visual editors that allow direct manipulation of web elements through drag-and-drop interfaces and conversational prompts. Design engineering has evolved from a bridge role during design-to-developer handoffs to an execution role where professionals can both design and code. Tools like Cursor's visual editor exemplify this shift by enabling users to "click on a thing, talk to it, and iterate," bringing design and engineering closer together.

Canva uses handcrafted animation to challenge polished design culture (4 minute read) Canva has launched a handcrafted stop-motion ad featuring a pigeon creatively rebranding an Italian trattoria to promote the idea that design should feel accessible rather than intimidating. Created with tactile handmade animation by DUDE Milan and former Aardman Studios animator Dan Richards, the campaign deliberately embraces imperfect, human-made visuals as a contrast to increasingly AI-driven creative tools — despite Canva itself heavily investing in AI features.

Zen and the art of illustration with Livelyhood (5 minute read) Thai illustrator Sornsil Apichirapokey says moving from busy Bangkok to the peaceful Satun region transformed his creativity, inspiring calm, mindfulness-focused illustrations shaped by Buddhism, nature, and sustainability.

Claude Design vs. Adobe Creative Cloud Pro: 2026 Comparison (17 minute read) Rather than direct substitutes, Claude Design and Adobe Creative Cloud Pro occupy opposite ends of a creative spectrum—Claude Design accelerating early-stage ideation, Adobe refining work to professional quality—and can even operate together via an Adobe connector that brings Photoshop and Firefly tools directly into Claude.
