### Assume everything you say at work is now recorded
- **URL:** https://www.a16z.news/p/everything-is-recorded-now?utm_source=tldrfounders
- **Via:** TLDR Founders, 2026-06-12
- **Read time:** 5 minute read
- **TLDR Summary:** AI notetakers quietly made recording the default, and the smart play is to treat it as permanent and onboard your AI like a new hire instead of a document. You don't hand a new employee the wiki and walk off - you put them in meetings, where the culture and the real judgment calls actually live. AI learns the same way, except it sits in every meeting and never gets bored. Talk-heavy companies like Shopify and OpenAI get a compounding edge because the context that used to evaporate in conversation finally gets captured.

### Venture's paper wealth is at a record, its bank accounts are dry

- **Via:** TLDR Founders, 2026-06-12
- **Read time:** 11 minute read

Look at the secondary market, where stakes change hands at 40 to 60% below their official marks, the real write-down happening where the quarterly letters won't show it.

### Why your AI bill keeps exploding
- **URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/7-deadly-sins-ai-spend-max-brodeur-urbas-unuic/?utm_source=tldrfounders
- **Via:** TLDR Founders, 2026-06-12
- **Read time:** 5 minute read
- **TLDR Summary:** Most AI bills balloon for a dumb reason: companies run frontier models for everything, when open-source models are now nearly as good and often 90% cheaper. One team swapped off Opus and cut costs by 93% with nobody noticing. The fastest win is routing: send only the genuinely hard work to the expensive model and push the rest to a cheap one, and the bill drops the same day. The other money pit is rewarding raw usage, because the minute you post a most-tokens-used leaderboard people burn cash to top it, so track spend per person and judge everyone on impact instead.

### In a confusing market, buyers buy your point of view
- **URL:** https://aprildunford.substack.com/p/in-the-age-of-ai-you-need-a-point?utm_source=tldrfounders
- **Via:** TLDR Founders, 2026-06-12
- **Read time:** 5 minute read
- **TLDR Summary:** When a market is changing fast, buyers stall because they're scared of picking something they'll regret in five years, and what reassures them is hearing your clear view of where the whole market is going. This is your belief about where things are heading, the assumption your roadmap rests on, and it should be rooted in what you do better than anyone. Notice how the giants each push a future that conveniently centers their own strength: OpenAI says AI is a metered utility, Microsoft says models are commodities and context is king, and ServiceNow says it's all workflows and governance. Stake out yours just as deliberately.
