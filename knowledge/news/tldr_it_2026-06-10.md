### Check Point VPN flaw is being exploited in the wild

### CIOs are responsible for AI systems they do not fully control
- **URL:** https://www.itpro.com/technology/artificial-intelligence/cios-and-ctos-are-making-high-stakes-decisions-with-incomplete-information-ibm-survey-reveals?utm_source=tldrit

- **TLDR Summary:** A survey by IBM found that two-thirds of CIOs and CTOs are accountable for AI systems they do not fully control, while only 11% feel fully prepared for large-scale AI deployment. ### Microsoft's pitch to enterprises: Ditch Azure Repos for GitHub, despite its rocky reliability record
- **URL:** https://thenewstack.io/azure-repos-github-migration/?utm_source=tldrit
- **Via:** TLDR IT, 2026-06-10
- **Read time:** 5 minute read
- **TLDR Summary:** Microsoft is urging enterprise customers to migrate from Azure Repos to GitHub, arguing that GitHub is the AI-native home for Copilot and agentic development workflows. It points to Enterprise Live Migrations (ELM) as a way to reduce downtime, keeping repos in sync and aiming for cutovers typically under 30 minutes, while acknowledging that migration involves more than just Git history and branches.

### PostgreSQL 19 Beta 1 is now available in Amazon RDS Database Preview Environment

- **Via:** TLDR IT, 2026-06-10
- **Read time:** 2 minute read
- **TLDR Summary:** Amazon has added PostgreSQL 19 Beta 1 to the Amazon RDS Database Preview Environment, so users can evaluate the pre-release PostgreSQL 19 on a fully managed service. ### Introducing Lightstep UQL to PromQL Translator

- **Via:** TLDR IT, 2026-06-10
