### We Analyzed 137K Sites: 97% of llms.txt Files Never Get Read

### Topics matter for third-party authority signals
- **URL:** https://www.growth-memo.com/p/topics-matter-for-third-party-authority?utm_source=tldrmarketing
- **Via:** TLDR Marketing, 2026-06-16
- **Read time:** 6 minute read
- **TLDR Summary:** AI pulls from a different set of trusted sources for every topic, making broad off-site authority building inefficient. Instead of spreading mentions across generic high-authority sites, brands need depth within the specific sources models already trust in their niche. AI reuses existing trust from those entities, meaning citations depend more on topical reputation and credible third-party mentions than overall backlink volume. Concentrated placements in a few top-tier, in-topic sources drive more visibility than scattered coverage.

### Quantifying YouTube Keyword Opportunities
- **URL:** https://moz.com/blog/quantifying-youtube-keyword-opportunities-whiteboard-friday?utm_source=tldrmarketing
- **Via:** TLDR Marketing, 2026-06-16
- **Read time:** 4 minute read
- **TLDR Summary:** YouTube keyword research uses different metrics than traditional SEO since most views come from discovery, not search. Instead of search volume, use Median Monthly View Velocity (MMVV) to estimate demand across top videos. Competition is measured by median subscribers of ranking channels, while median video age shows how long content stays relevant. Keywords with strong view velocity, lower subscriber competition, and longer lifespan signal more potential.
