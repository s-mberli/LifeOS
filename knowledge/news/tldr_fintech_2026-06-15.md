### Ramp launches private SWE-Bench for financial coding agents
- **URL:** https://labs.ramp.com/swebench?utm_source=tldrfintech
- **Via:** TLDR Fintech, 2026-06-15
- **Read time:** 3 minute read
- **TLDR Summary:** Ramp SWE-Bench is a private benchmark built from 80 production backend tasks that Ramp's internal coding agent, Inspect, previously shipped after engineer review. The benchmark evaluates how background coding agents handle financial software work across areas like card authorization, bill pay, accounting, procurement, treasury, and fraud. Pass/fail scoring is based on whether the agent produces a review-ready patch that flips tests from failing to passing within 45 minutes.

### Systems of record won the SaaS era, clearinghouses will win the agents era
- **URL:** https://cloudedjudgement.substack.com/p/systems-of-record-won-the-saas-era?utm_source=tldrfintech
- **Via:** TLDR Fintech, 2026-06-15
- **Read time:** 3 minute read
- **TLDR Summary:** The AI-era equivalent of the SaaS system of record is the “clearinghouse” for agents: the layer that governs what agents know, see, do, and prove after the fact. Durable moats will shift from owning data to owning permissions, policy, execution controls, and audit trails, creating opportunities for vertical startups or neutral multi-agent governance platforms that can sit between Microsoft, Salesforce, Snowflake, Databricks, and agent-native tools.
