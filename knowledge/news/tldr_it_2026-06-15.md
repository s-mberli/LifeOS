### US orders Anthropic to suspend access to Fable 5 and Mythos 5

- **TLDR Summary:** The US government ordered Anthropic to block foreign nationals from accessing its most advanced models, Fable 5 and Mythos 5, citing national security concerns. ### Systems of Record Won the SaaS Era - Clearinghouses Will Win the Agents Era

- **Via:** TLDR IT, 2026-06-15

### Why CISOs Must Re-Think Identity Posture Management for AI Agents
- **URL:** https://softwareanalyst.substack.com/p/why-cisos-must-re-think-identity?utm_source=tldrit
- **Via:** TLDR IT, 2026-06-15
- **Read time:** 15 minute read
- **TLDR Summary:** Most enterprise Identity Security Posture Management (ISPM) programs are structurally mismatched to today's threat environment, especially as AI agents enter workflows with identities and entitlements. An “Agentic ISPM” phase would allow posture findings to translate into closed-loop, policy-bounded remediation: propose, approve (if required), execute, verify, and provide auditable evidence without waiting for manual tickets.

### Your API Is Already a Hidden MCP Server, You Just Need to Discover It
- **URL:** https://shiftmag.dev/api-mcp-server-infobip-filip-srnec-10118/?utm_source=tldrit
- **Via:** TLDR IT, 2026-06-15
- **Read time:** 6 minute read
