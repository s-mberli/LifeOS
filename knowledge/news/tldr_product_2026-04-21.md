The AI Chasm (10 minute read) AI adoption looks fast but remains shallow, with most companies far from real impact. The real opportunity is internal use and adapting how teams work.

Your AI agent can read your codebase. It doesn't know your product (12 minute read) AI understands code, not product taste. To get better outputs, you must explicitly give it your design context.

How to hire people who are better than you (10 minute read) Hire beyond your expertise by judging how candidates think, not what they know. The right person teaches you, offers specific ideas, and elevates the whole company.

Start designing in content, not in code (5 minute read) Start with content, not code, to avoid bloated, confusing products. Clear thinking about what users need leads to better design than rushing into features.

Agentic coordination, Human delivery (9 minute read) AI should automate coordination, not ownership or execution. Removing middle-layer friction helps teams move faster and understand their work better.

What's New With Product Positioning? (73 minute podcast) Positioning defines why customers pick you now and must reflect real value. It's a hypothesis tested through sales, not just messaging.

The shape of leadership (8 minute read) Leadership in the AI era is about setting vision, maintaining quality, and managing bets, not directing work. Great leaders use the tools, filter noise, and make bold decisions under uncertainty.

Why bizware is becoming the dominant form of software (9 minute read) Software has been divided into deep engineering and “bizware” that powers business infrastructure. AI thrives in the repetitive, system-building layer, not frontier innovation.

Strong opinions, loosely held — and what that means in the age of AI (2 minute read) AI can generate ideas, but you still need to form and test your own opinions.

The Work Runs on Different Maps (3 minute read) Execution runs on hidden maps like expertise and trust, not just org charts.
