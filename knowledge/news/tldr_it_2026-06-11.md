### The Ontology Engineering Challenge is Not Building It, but Mirroring the Delta
- **URL:** https://moderndata101.substack.com/p/minimal-ontology-principle?utm_source=tldrit
- **Via:** TLDR IT, 2026-06-11
- **Read time:** 10 minute read
- **TLDR Summary:** The key enterprise AI challenge lies not in building an ontology, but in identifying where an LLM's latent understanding of business concepts diverges from a company's definitions and correcting only those gaps. An LLM develops a “latent ontology” through geometric relationships in embedding space, while companies define a curated “structural ontology” through semantic layer metrics and business definitions. Misalignment between the two creates source-of-truth errors in agentic systems.
