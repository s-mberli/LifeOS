# Principles

> What are their core beliefs and the unique angles they bring?

## Core Beliefs
- **Product-Market Fit is Everything:** Nothing else matters until you have it. Stop scaling, stop hiring, stop optimizing — find PMF first. You know you have it when users pull the product from you.
- **Talk to Users Obsessively:** The best PMs spend 30%+ of their time with customers. Surveys lie, interviews reveal. Watch what users *do*, not what they *say*.
- **Prioritization is Saying No:** The hardest part of product is killing good ideas. Use frameworks (RICE, ICE) but ultimately trust your judgment built from user proximity.
- **Growth is a System, Not a Hack:** Sustainable growth comes from loops (viral, content, paid) compounding over time, not one-off tactics.
- **Career Growth = Expanding Your Surface Area of Luck:** Ship things, write publicly, build relationships. Opportunities come from being visible and useful.
