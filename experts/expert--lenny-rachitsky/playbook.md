# Playbook

> Actionable advice, tactics, and strategies extracted from their insights.

## On Product-Market Fit
- **The Sean Ellis Test:** Ask users "How would you feel if you could no longer use this product?" If 40%+ say "very disappointed," you have PMF.
- **Do Things That Don't Scale:** Manually onboard your first 100 users. Hand-hold them. The insights from this phase are irreplaceable.
- **Find Your Desperate Users:** Don't build for "everyone." Find the people who *need* your product most urgently and delight them completely.

## On Prioritization & Strategy
- **The "Magic Wand" Question:** Ask your team: "If you had a magic wand and could ship one thing instantly, what would it be?" Cuts through analysis paralysis.
- **Write a One-Pager First:** Before building anything, write a 1-page strategy doc: Problem, Solution, Why Now, Success Metrics. If you can't fill this page, you're not ready.
- **Kill Features Ruthlessly:** Track feature usage. If <5% of users touch it after 3 months, consider removing it.

## On Growth
- **Identify Your Growth Loop:** Every great product has one dominant growth loop (viral, content, paid, sales). Find yours and pour fuel on it.
- **Activation is the Most Underrated Metric:** Most growth problems are actually activation problems. Focus on getting new users to their "aha moment" faster.
- **Referral Programs Work When the Product is Already Great:** Don't use referrals to compensate for a mediocre product.

## On Career & Leadership
- **Become a "Learn-It-All" Not a "Know-It-All":** The best PMs are relentlessly curious. Read widely, talk to people outside your domain.
- **Write Publicly:** Publishing your thinking attracts mentors, collaborators, and opportunities you can't predict.
- **Give Away Your Best Ideas:** Generosity compounds. The more you share, the more you become a node in valuable networks.
