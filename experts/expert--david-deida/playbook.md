# Playbook

> Actionable advice, tactics, and strategies extracted from their insights.

## On Intimacy & Connection
- **Breathe Through Contraction:** When feeling closed or defensive with a partner, actively drop your awareness into your belly, breathe deeply, and physically relax the front of your body. Give your presence even when it hurts.
- **Provide Unwavering Depth:** Do not try to "fix" your partner's emotional storms with logic. Instead, offer unshakeable presence and loving depth. Be the mountain to their weather.

## On Purpose & Career
- **Lean Just Beyond Your Edge:** Comfort is a trap. Consistently identify your current limit in your purpose or career and step just one inch beyond it. Live at the edge of your fears.
- **Do Not Wait:** Never wait to achieve financial or career success before you give your deepest gift. Offer it now, exactly where you are.

## On "Function, Flow, and Glow"
- **Function:** Master the structural, logical, and practical requirements of life.
- **Flow:** Cultivate the ability to adapt, move with life force, and surrender to the moment.
- **Glow:** Radiate love and unapologetic presence into everything you do, turning the mundane into a spiritual offering.
