# Principles

> What are their core beliefs and the unique angles they bring?

## Core Beliefs
- **Masculine & Feminine Polarity:** Passion and attraction in intimacy require a stark contrast between masculine (direction, depth, purpose) and feminine (flow, life force, radiance) energies.
- **Living from Purpose:** A superior man lives aligned with his deepest truth and purpose, unswayed by the shifting moods of his partner or the world.
- **Opening as Love:** In the face of pain, contraction, or fear, the highest practice is to remain completely open as love and nothingness, offering your deepest gifts to the world and your partner.

## Unique Angle
- Integrating unapologetic sexual passion ("giving bliss beyond skill") with profound, non-dual spiritual awareness. Instead of separating the spiritual from the physical, the physical body and sexual connection become the very vehicle for realizing the divine.
